Our People

A Visionary Partnership

Founded by a mother-and-son duo, Innocode blends high-level technical expertise with entrepreneurial heart — united by a shared mission to empower Sabah's community through technology.

Co-Founder & Technologist
Bryan Lee
Lead of Software Innovation & Education

A 2019 Computer Science graduate from the University of Utah, Bryan brings a global perspective to Sabah's tech scene. As a specialist software engineer and game developer, he has a proven track record of delivering high-quality software solutions for both agile startups and established corporations.

Bryan's deep understanding of code isn't just about building programs — it's about solving problems. His transition into education stems from a desire to demystify complex technologies, ensuring the next generation of creators has the tools to build the future.

University of Utah · CS 2019
Co-Founder & Mentor
Dr. Sallie Tan
Director of Business Development & Life Coaching

An alumna of Berkeley International University, Dr. Sallie Tan is a visionary entrepreneur and the founder of Gloria Perfect Aesthetic Sdn Bhd. Through the years, she has cemented her reputation as a successful businesswoman and dedicated life and beauty coach.

Dr. Sallie is more than just an entrepreneur — she is a connector. Known for her unwavering support of aspiring business owners, she believes that success is best when shared. Her influence ensures that Innocode is a launchpad for professional growth and confidence.

Berkeley International University
AI Programme Lead
Scholena Kinit Leon
Facilitator of AI Technology Integration

A Forestry Science graduate from Universiti Malaysia Sabah (UMS) who views the tropical rainforest as the world's most advanced technological system. Scholena is dedicated to bridging the digital gap by bringing AI education to the grassroots level — with no age barriers.

By blending local wisdom with digital innovation, Scholena focuses on developing ethical and responsible AI users who view technology as a tool for restoration, not destruction.
